KNOWBRIDGE

Keep a project’s knowledge when someone leaves. Demo data is invented.

What is KNOWBRIDGE?

When someone leaves a project or club, much of what they knew leaves with them: why decisions were made, who owns what, and what is unfinished. KNOWBRIDGE reads the project’s files, lists the important points, shows the exact line each point came from as proof, lets a reviewer approve them, and shows the next person only what their role may see.

Try it in 4 steps

  1. Click Start the demo. You are signed in as the Reviewer.
  2. Click Load demo data, then Extract claims.
  3. Open Review, click any point to see its proof, then click Approve all verified.
  4. Click Sign out, sign in as Incoming non-technical member and open Handover. The “Restricted” boxes are on purpose.

Extra: sign in as the Departing member to answer the questions the system asks about gaps in the files (Interview tab). The Reviewer then approves those answers in Review.

All data is invented. Passwords, API keys and phone numbers in uploaded files are masked automatically.
